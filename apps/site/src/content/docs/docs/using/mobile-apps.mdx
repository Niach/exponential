Exponential runs natively on iOS 17.4+ and Android 8+, synced in real time with the web and desktop apps, across all your teams.

<LinkButton href="https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=at.exponential" variant="secondary">Google Play</LinkButton>

## Getting around

The bar along the bottom holds **Chats**, **Teams**, the Exponential logo, **New** and **Settings**. The logo opens your Assistant. **New** files an issue or starts a chat. Chats, Teams and Settings have round search and inbox buttons, which cover every team.

<PlatformShots view="teams" platforms={["ios", "android"]} />

**Teams** lists your teams. A team holds its boards, agents and apps and the chats you placed there, in your own order and folders from the desktop sidebar. Its **…** menu creates a board, agent or app and opens the team's settings.

<PlatformShots view="team" platforms={["ios", "android"]} />

## The board

<PlatformShots view="board" platforms={["ios", "android"]} />

A board has **All issues**, **My issues** and **Drafts** under its title. Long-press an issue for its menu, or select several and the bulk bar takes the bar's place.

## Steer from anywhere

<PlatformShots view="chats" platforms={["ios", "android"]} />

**Chats** lists your agents on top, then your chats from every team under **Needs you**, **Working**, **Unread** and **Chats**. The **Needs you** row opens every plan, question and PR ready to merge in one list. Open a chat to watch the agent work live and steer it: answer its question, approve a plan, add a constraint. The feed and your messages travel straight between the phone and the machine running the session, end-to-end encrypted.

<PlatformShots view="steering" platforms={["ios", "android"]} />

A chat is one screen paged by its strip: the **Conversation**, each linked issue and each session's work, then **Results**, what the agent showed you. A plan opens on the chat's **Plan** tab. The top bar carries **Merge PR** while the pull request is open, and **Stop** ends the chat. Long-press a chat in the list to stop or delete it.

You can start sessions here too: the composer's **+** menu, `/` for prompts, `@` for a teammate and `#` for an issue work as on the desktop. The full flow is in [Sessions](/docs/using/sessions/).

## Push notifications

<PlatformShots view="inbox" platforms={["ios", "android"]} />

Everything that lands in your inbox (assignments, comments, mentions, status changes, PRs, feedback, reporter replies, and questions or plans from your chats) arrives as a push. Tapping one opens the issue or chat. **Settings → Notifications** applies to push too.

<Aside>The store apps push only for cloud accounts. A self-hosted server reaches you through web, desktop and email notifications instead: see [Self-host](/docs/get-started/self-host/#limitations).</Aside>
