The desktop app runs your [sessions](/docs/using/sessions/) and the git tools around them, on macOS, Windows and Linux. Sign in to the cloud or to your [self-hosted](/docs/get-started/self-host/) server.

<LinkButton href="/download/">Download</LinkButton>

It updates itself in the background and restarts once its sessions pause between turns. **Restart to update** in the banner does it at once.

## Notifications

<PlatformShots view="settings-notifications" platforms={["desktop"]} />

Whatever lands in your inbox also arrives as a system notification. A burst arrives as one, and nothing pops up for an issue you already have open. **Settings → Notifications → Desktop notifications** switches them for this computer, and the switches for each type apply too.

## Voice

<PlatformShots view="settings-assistant" platforms={["desktop"]} />

The desktop your Assistant runs on is your voice device: it listens and speaks for you. Its tray holds push to talk, the wake word and a call to your Assistant. See [Voice](/docs/using/voice/).
