Your Assistant answers out loud, in English or German. Everything runs on your own desktop: the one your Assistant runs on. Voice is in beta.

## Set it up

<PlatformShots view="settings-assistant" platforms={["desktop"]} />

In **Settings → Assistant**, pick one of your desktops as the **Device** under **Runs on**. Its **Voice** section holds the language and, optionally, a clip of [your voice](/docs/sdk/voice/guides/your-voice/). The desktop downloads the voice on first use; it needs a GPU ([requirements](/docs/sdk/voice/guides/install/)).

## Call it

<PlatformShots view="voice-call" platforms={["web"]} />

The phone in your Assistant's chat calls it, from the web, your phone or the voice device itself. Talk over a reply to interrupt it. On a phone the call keeps going with the screen locked.

## Push to talk

<PlatformShots view="voice-call-overlay" platforms={["desktop"]} />

On the voice device, hold a shortcut, speak and let go. Record the shortcut under **On `<device>` (tray)** in the same section, or turn on the wake word and say its phrase. [Shortcuts per platform](/docs/sdk/voice/guides/push-to-talk/).
