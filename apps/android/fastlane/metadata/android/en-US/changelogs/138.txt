- Start: the app opens on the Agent page, and the chat button lights up while you are there
- Tabs: Inbox, My Issues and Drafts swipe sideways, and so do Actions and Suggestions
- Reviews: a pull request from a chat or action run opens its changes in the app
- Runs: an arrow jumps to the newest message and the task list shows its progress
- New issue: one tap on the × discards a draft
