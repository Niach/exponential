Chats: threads are chats; the bottom bar is Board, Devices, Chats and Team, and the Chats tab lists your team's chats like a messenger, agents as bubbles on top.
Long-press a chat to stop it or stop and delete it.
Agents: Compact and New conversation in the chat menu, or /compact and /new; agents draw live previews into the chat.
One active team at a time, switched on the Team tab; My issues and Drafts are chips on the board; the inbox has its own page.
Agents can wake every 5 to 30 minutes. Apps lists only your team's apps.
