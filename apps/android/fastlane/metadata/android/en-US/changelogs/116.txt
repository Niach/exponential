Sign in with a code mailed to you or with a passkey: every option is one Continue list. The working indicator follows the agent's real turn and ends when the agent does. Exponential tool calls render as cards with a result preview, and multi-agent runs get a workflow card plus a caption on the session row. Account health comes from a live probe, so a dead login says Needs re-login, and a Claude run at its limit can switch accounts. Leaving a session returns you where you came from.
