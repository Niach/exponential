One Work screen: an issue, its coding run, the changes and the run's screenshots are faces of one screen, switched from the bar at the bottom, with Stop or Resume top right. Every diff now reads the same, with hidden lines you can open. An issue blocked by another can be started on top of it and the chain merged in one go. Batch runs are named after the issues they cover, drafts keep an unfinished issue, issue badges show their status again, and connecting GitHub matches the desktop.
