Issue, Run, Changes and Results are swipeable tabs in the header, with Merge beside them while the pull request is open. Change your email and link or unlink sign-in methods under Settings > Account. Merging a stacked pull request asks: the stack or this pull request only. A run's report sits beside its screenshots, tall screenshots scroll and pinch to zoom, notifications can open Results.
