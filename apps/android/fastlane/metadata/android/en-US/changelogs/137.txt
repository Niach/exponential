- GitHub: one page connects your account, installs the app and lists your repositories
- Feedback: a widget report is an issue; reply to the reporter from the composer
- New issue: a full page, saved as a draft while you type
- Navigation: an Actions tab; lists span every team you belong to
- Pull requests: the diff is the issue's Changes tab
- Runs: the agent's mark spins while it works, amber waits on you, green has a pull request open
