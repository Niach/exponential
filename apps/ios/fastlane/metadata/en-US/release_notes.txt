- Work screen: Issue, Run, Changes and Results are swipeable tabs in the header, with Merge beside them while the pull request is open
- Settings > Account: change your email with a code, link or unlink Google, Apple and OIDC logins, and manage passkeys
- Merging a stacked pull request asks whether to merge the whole stack or this pull request only
- Results: a run's report sits beside its screenshots, tall screenshots open in a scrolling viewer, and a notification can open Results directly
- Markdown attachments open in an in-app preview
- Toasts: one stacking notice style everywhere, tap to expand, swipe to dismiss
- Tidy up: a built-in action for board cleanup, with matching icons for stacks, batches and workflows
- Sub-issues composed with an attachment no longer leave a draft behind
