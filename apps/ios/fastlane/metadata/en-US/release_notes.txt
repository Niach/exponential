- Chats: threads are now chats; the bottom bar is Board, Devices, Chats and Team, and the Chats tab lists your team's chats like a messenger, with agents as bubbles on top
- Long-press a chat to stop it or stop and delete it; an ended chat offers Delete
- Agents: Compact conversation and New conversation in an agent chat's menu, or type /compact and /new
- Live previews: agents can draw an interactive preview straight into the chat
- Plans: a waiting plan always shows in the chat with its buttons, and a newer plan replaces the waiting one
- One active team at a time, switched on the Team tab, which also holds the settings; My issues and Drafts are chips on the board, and the inbox has its own page
- Triggers: agents can wake every 5, 10, 15, 20 or 30 minutes
- Apps lists only your team's apps; the built-in Devices app is gone
- Messages load through their conversation, ready for direct messages and channels
