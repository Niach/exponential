- Todo and Backlog are now one list: everything that sat in Todo lives in Backlog
- Sheets and menus share one uniform look across the app
- The editor keeps your intentional blank lines, identically on every platform
- Agents always run without permission prompts; the toggle is gone
- One agent picker: agent, model, effort, plan mode, account and usage in a single card
- Actions: create and edit actions with their inputs right on your phone
- Reviews: a new list and PR diff with per-file cards; Fix merge conflicts replaces Merge when a merge hits a conflict
- Navigation badges are dots: Inbox and Support on unread, Devices while a run is live, Reviews while a PR is open
- Steering: plans are no longer cut off or shown twice, and a finished session closes its view
- Starting a run on an offline machine is refused right away instead of being silently queued
