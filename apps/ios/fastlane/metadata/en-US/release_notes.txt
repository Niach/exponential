- Speak replies (beta, English and German): pick your voice device in Settings > Voice and let the IDE read your agent's replies aloud, in a built-in voice or one cloned from your own clip
- Settings > Voice is yours across devices: voice device, agent, Speak replies, Push to talk and its shortcut, the wake phrase, Language (English or Deutsch), a voice file and its transcript; the same switches sit in your voice device's system tray
- Coming next: calling your agent from the phone, and the wake word
- Voice switches stay off after their agent is deleted
- A long turn no longer shows "needs input" by mistake, and sessions on a shared machine are listed under the right host
