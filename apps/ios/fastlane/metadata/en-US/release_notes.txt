- GitHub: one guided page connects your GitHub account, installs the app and lists the repositories you can push to
- Feedback: a widget submission is an issue; reply to the reporter from the comment composer and their answers come back as comments
- New issue: creating an issue opens a full page with every property, saved as a draft while you type
- Navigation: Inbox, Devices, Reviews, Agent and Actions tabs; Reviews, runs, actions and devices list every team you belong to
- Pull requests: the diff is the issue's Changes face, Results reads as a numbered guide, Merge sits on the bottom bar
- Runs: the agent's mark spins while it works, amber waits on you, green has a pull request open, blue is done
- Issue screen: the title collapses into the header as you scroll and content slides under a blurred edge
- Joining a team: the invite flow offers the device setup step when you own no device yet
