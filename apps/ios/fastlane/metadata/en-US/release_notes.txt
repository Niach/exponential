- Apps keep their own records and read the team: boards, members, chats, comments and files
- Apps act as you: each action asks first, unless a team owner granted it on the app's settings page
- Export an app as one file and import it in New app by pasting it or choosing the file
- Bottom bar: Board, Chats, Apps and Team; slide a finger across it to pick a tab
- The Board tab opens a menu of the team's boards, with New board at the end
- Every page has the same big title with search and inbox; each setting opens its own page
- Search looks in the active team only and keeps up with your typing
- Long chats open instantly
- Agents can be assigned issues and show under their own name; mentions show as pills
- New board on an empty board creates the board in the team you are looking at
