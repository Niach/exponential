- Threads: every piece of work is one conversation, listed as Needs you, Running and Recent, with each session as a card
- Team agents: add agents in Settings that look after a board from their own device and start a thread for each piece of work
- Shared threads: a thread on a device shared with the team is visible to the whole team, who can reply, answer and stop it
- Actions are saved prompts, and merging a pull request no longer ends a session
