- Actions: automations are now triggers on the action they run, and each action is a page with its prompt, its triggers and its runs
- Work screen: swipe between Issue, Run, Changes and Results, and Changes shows its line counts once the files are known
- Runs: an API error shows as its own row in the transcript instead of a false usage-limit wall
