Everything since 0.14.0:

Coding sessions
- Answer agent permission and approval prompts right from your phone instead of waiting until you are back at the machine
- Attach screenshots and images to a live coding session
- Start coding on several issues at once (batch runs) from the Start coding dialog, which now pre-fills the model, effort, plan mode and permission defaults of the machine you pick
- Sessions show as paused while their machine is offline, and resume from anywhere
- Sign the agent CLI in remotely when a session asks for it
- Resolve merge conflicts from your phone with the Fix merge conflicts action
- Long activity feeds no longer freeze the app, and the Jump to bottom pill hides reliably at the end of the feed
- Free-text steering replies are capped at 4000 characters with a visible counter instead of being dropped silently

Actions and automations
- Give an action a schedule or an issue-event trigger and one of your machines runs it on its own
- Tabbed Actions surface with automations, suggestions and a uniform layout

Machines
- Every machine gets a settings view: rename it, share it with your team, edit its agent defaults even while it is offline, and manage its worktrees remotely
- Machines with an outdated daemon show a one-click update button

Writing
- Emoji picker and :shortcode autocomplete in descriptions and comments
- Attach images and files to comments
- A formatting rail in the editor; Clear formatting and heading toggles keep your bold and italic marks
- Typing # opens an issue menu with status, identifier and title, and issue references render as live status chips

Issues and boards
- Assignee picker on the issue page
- Archive boards you are done with, and rename them in place
- Issue lists group statuses in the same order as your settings page and pin their status headers cleanly
- Issue lists sit flush under the navigation bar on iOS 26, no more light band above the first status header
- Compact icon swatch for board and action icons
- A getting-started checklist that finishes itself

Team settings
- Connect your own GitHub repos and share them with the team; stale GitHub accounts get a Disconnect button
- Team and account settings on one page, with a self-service section for personal API keys

Also
- Panels, lists and tabs animate with one shared timing
- Fixed being logged out on every relaunch after an update
- A wave of stability, performance and correctness fixes across the app
