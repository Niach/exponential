Exponential is a realtime workspace for teams that build with AI — issues, customer support, and coding agents in one place, live on every device.

From report to shipped:
• A visitor files a report through your feedback widget — screenshot, annotations, and page context attached automatically.
• The report lands as an issue on your board, in real time, on every device.
• Pick an issue and hit Start coding — even from your phone. A coding agent works on a real branch in your desktop IDE and opens the pull request when it's done.
• You review and merge — and the original reporter gets an email that their issue is resolved.

Bring your own agents. Coding agents run locally on your connected desktop, on your own Claude or Codex subscription — unlimited sessions, flat price.

Native everywhere. The iOS app syncs live with the web and desktop apps over the same real-time engine, so your board is always current — no pull-to-refresh, no stale views.

Built for the way you actually work:
• Fast issue tracking with priorities, labels, due dates, and custom statuses.
• Boards you can back with a GitHub repository to code on.
• Rich Markdown descriptions and comments with @mentions, task lists, and inline images.
• Watch coding sessions live in the Agents tab, steer them, and answer your agent's questions right from your phone.
• Saved actions — reusable agent prompts your team runs with one tap, on any connected desktop.
• A shared Support inbox — helpdesk tickets from your users land next to your issues, and replies go out by email.
• Push notifications for mentions, assignments, and PR activity.

Free for teams of up to three on the hosted cloud. Exponential is open source under Apache-2.0 — self-hosting is free for everyone, at any company size.
