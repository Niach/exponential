Vibecode together.

Exponential is a realtime issue tracker for teams that build with AI. Issues, customer support and coding agents in one workspace, in sync on every device.

Track work with your team:
• Issues with priorities, labels, due dates and custom statuses
• Boards for every part of your product, each optionally backed by a GitHub repository
• Markdown descriptions and comments with @mentions, task lists and inline images
• Realtime sync across web, desktop, iOS and Android. No refresh, no stale views
• Push notifications for mentions, assignments and PR activity

Bring your own agents. Claude Code, Codex or pi run locally on your connected desktop, on your subscription. Pick an issue and hit Start coding, even from your phone. Your agent works on a real branch in your desktop IDE and opens the pull request when it is done.

• Watch coding sessions live in the Agents tab and steer them by message
• Answer your agent's questions right from your phone
• Save actions for repeating AI tasks like code reviews, deploys or merge conflicts, and run them with one tap

Customer support in the same workspace. Tickets from your users land next to your issues, replies go out by email, and the whole team sees the conversation.

Free for teams of up to three on the hosted cloud, one plan when you grow. Exponential is open source under Apache-2.0, so self-hosting is free at any company size.
