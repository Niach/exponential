There are two ways to sign in — either works, and all functionality is available on both paths.

1) Recommended (no credentials needed): tap "Continue with Apple" and use your own Apple ID. The account is created automatically; there is no password step. You then land on a short wizard: "Set up your team" (type any name and tap "Create team"; you become its owner), then "Create your first board" (enter a name, e.g. "Inbox"; connecting a GitHub repository is optional — skip it). You are then in the app: create an issue with "+", open it to set status, priority, labels, and due date, and add comments.

2) Optional, to see a fully populated team: tap "Continue with Google" and sign in with the demo account below. It opens the pre-seeded "Acme" team with boards, issues, comments, and the Support/helpdesk surface. If Google shows a "verify it's you" challenge on your device, simply use path (1) instead — nothing is gated behind the demo account.

Notes:
- The instance URL field on the first screen exists because Exponential is source-available and can be self-hosted — the cloud is the default and needs no input.
- Coding sessions on the Agents tab are started from our desktop app. With no desktop online the tab shows a "No desktop online" hint and a "No agents running" empty state — this is expected and not a bug.
- Teammates are invited from the web app, not from iOS.
- There are no purchases, subscriptions, or paid features in the iOS app; billing exists only on the web.

Account deletion (guideline 5.1.1(v)) is in the app, no website visit needed:
1. On the Issues screen, tap the gear icon at the top right to open Settings.
2. Under "Servers", tap your account row ("Exponential Cloud", your email below it).
3. Tap "Delete account" (red, directly below "Sign out").
4. Confirm in the "Delete your account?" alert.
The account and its data are deleted immediately on the server, together with any team
where you are the only member, and the app returns to the signed-out screen. No support
contact or website visit is involved at any point.
